# Lecture Live

Record a lecture on your Mac and get a live transcript, then study notes, quizzes and flashcards from it. The transcript is made on your Mac. Notes and answers come from Claude, using your own API key.

**[Download Lecture Live for Mac](https://github.com/Anderson00402/lecture-live-download/releases/latest/download/Lecture-Live.zip)** (about 40 MB)

## What you need

- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). Intel Macs aren't supported.
- macOS 13 Ventura or newer.
- About 2 GB of free space for the speech model the app downloads the first time.
- A Claude API key from [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com), for notes, quizzes and the assistant.
  - What you use is billed to your key, pay as you go.
  - Recording and the live transcript work without a key.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded zip and drag **Lecture Live** into your Applications folder.
2. Open Lecture Live. macOS says it can't verify the app, because the app isn't notarized by Apple. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings**, then **Privacy & Security**. Scroll down to the message that Lecture Live was blocked, click **Open Anyway**, and confirm with your password.
   On macOS 13 or 14 you can instead right-click the app, choose **Open**, then click **Open** again.

You only do this once. After that, Lecture Live opens like any other app.

## The first time you open it

1. Create your account. The first account on a Mac is its admin.
2. Pick a speech model to download. Large v3 Turbo is the most accurate.
3. Paste your Claude API key.
4. Press **Record**, pick the course and a title, and start. macOS asks to use the microphone the first time: allow it.

## Good to know

- **Your recordings stay on your Mac**, in the `Lectures` folder in your home folder.
  - The audio is transcribed on the Mac and never uploaded.
  - The transcript's text goes to Anthropic (Claude) when you make notes or quizzes, or ask the assistant.
- **Check that you're allowed to record.** Many schools have a recording policy, and some professors ask students to check with them first.
- **To update,** download the newest version from this page and replace the app in Applications. Your lectures, notes and settings are kept.
